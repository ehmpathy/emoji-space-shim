## D1 — practice bug

- **practice**: `src/practices/provision-github/bad-practices/implicit-package-privacy/package.json.declapract.ts` (ehmpathy/declapract-typescript-ehmpathy, origin/main)
  - `check` detects a `package.json` whose `private` is absent or non-boolean
  - `fix` → `asPackageJsonWithPrivacyExplicit` writes `private: true` (line 48: `key === 'private' ? true : input.pkg[key]`)
- **the bug**: for `useCase: npm-package`, the fail-closed default guarantees harm: `npm publish` refuses (`.publish-npm.yml` runs `npm publish --access public`), and `provision/github.repo/resources.ts` maps it to `visibility: 'private'`
- **prior art**: same defect recorded in `ehmpathy/as-command` route `v2026_11_16.declapract.upgrade` (3.2 yield, D2b) and in `.dream/v2026_11_16.reseed.declapract-privacy-backfill-npm-package.md`. upstream fulcrum F110 chose `true` knowingly ("a published npm-package adopter then needs `"private": false` to publish")
- **suggested fix upstream**: for `useCase: npm-package` with a publish workflow, backfill `"private": false`, or fail the upgrade with a ConstraintError that asks the owner to choose, rather than a silent write
